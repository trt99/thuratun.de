What I can do for your team

Payments are my specialty, but I test other kinds of software too. I work alongside developers and product owners, on a single release or as a regular part of your sprints.

Payment testing

Checkout flows, declines, refunds and timeouts, tested with real cards and bank accounts from many issuers. I also have experience with on-site payment testing.

Manual and automated testing

Exploratory testing that catches what scripts miss, plus repeatable checks for the flows that must never break.

Planning and bug reports

A clear plan of what to test first, and reproducible bug reports with evidence attached.

Why my IT background helps

I also have a background in IT administration, so I know how systems, networks and access work. When a test fails, I can often tell whether it’s a bug in the software, a configuration issue or an environment problem. That saves your developers time.

How a testing project runs

The same four steps every time, so you always know where things stand.

  1. UnderstandWe agree on what the software should do and what matters most.
  2. TestI work through the plan and explore beyond it.
  3. ReportYou get findings ranked by severity, each one reproducible.
  4. RetestI verify the fixes before the release goes out.
